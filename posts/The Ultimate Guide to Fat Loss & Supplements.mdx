# Why Diets Fail (And What to Do Instead)
Most people fail at losing weight not because they aren't working hard, but because they are focusing on the wrong things. Crash diets destroy your metabolism. 

Here are the **three golden rules** of fat loss:
1. Eat in a slight caloric deficit.
2. Prioritize protein in every meal.
3. Be consistent.

<Alert type="info">Note: You do not have to starve yourself. Eating the right foods will keep you completely full while still losing fat!</Alert>
